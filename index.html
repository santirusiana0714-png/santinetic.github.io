ABOUT ME

Marketing gives ideas direction. Film gives them a voice.

I am Santino Rusiana, a digital marketing strategist and filmmaker working at the intersection of marketing, storytelling, and visual production.

After graduating from Boston University with a BSBA in Marketing in 2031, I began my career in a marketing agency, working across digital campaigns, branded content, and creative production.

I eventually found myself drawn toward the part of marketing I enjoyed most: turning an idea into something people could actually experience.

MY PHILOSOPHY

DON'T START WHAT YOU WON'T SEE FINISHED.

I believe in finishing what I start.

Creating isn't only about having a good idea. It's about having the discipline to carry that idea from the first thought to the final frame.

I believe good marketing should give people a reason to participate. I believe good filmmaking should come from the heart and shouldn't be restricted by unnecessary boundaries.

I care about the details that other people might overlook. I will spend more time on a project if I believe it can be better. When something goes wrong, I don't see it as a reason to stop. I see it as something that needs to be solved.

START WITH PURPOSE. WORK WITH EVERYTHING. FINISH WHAT YOU STARTED.
